This wizard creates a new attack that cures or increases a stat by a fixed amount when used out of battle.

Select the stat to modify (HP, MP, etc.) and the amount to increase it by, then select "Create Attack" to create the attack and assign it to this item.

For HP and MP, you can also choose whether the cure can exceed the target's maximum stat value.

The created attack uses Pure Damage with no randomization, and has appropriate bitsets set. You can edit it further in the Attack Editor if needed.
